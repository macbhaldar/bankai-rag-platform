# Securities Valuation Manual v7.9
Effective Date: 2026-04-23
Domain: Investments
Jurisdiction: UK

## Purpose
This synthetic banking document defines procedures for trade finance activities. It is intended for internal search, QA and RAG evaluation.

## Eligibility
Requests require KYC completion, collateral valuation, and exception approval. Additional conditions may apply based on customer risk, product characteristics, jurisdiction and delegated authority.

## Process
Staff must validate source records, apply the current approved methodology, record material decisions, and escalate exceptions before execution.

## Controls
Maker-checker controls, access restrictions, reconciliation, audit trails and periodic quality assurance are required where applicable.

## Approval Authority
Transactions outside delegated thresholds require escalation to the designated regional or central authority. Overrides require documented rationale and appropriate approval.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented justification, risk assessment, approval and expiry/review date.

## Monitoring
Control owners review exceptions, breaches, aging items and data-quality issues at defined intervals.

## Records and Retention
Supporting evidence must be retained according to the applicable records schedule and remain retrievable for audit.

## Escalation
Questions involving regulatory interpretation, suspected financial crime, material control failures or unresolved data-quality defects must be routed to the appropriate control function.
